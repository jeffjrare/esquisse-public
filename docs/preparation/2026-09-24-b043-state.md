## Instructions versus observed improvement

`/esq:status` now asks for projected order/text and live status separately, routes on
live facts, and does not infer staleness from closed membership. That is an instruction
correction, supported by a targeted reading of its field, display and next-action
paths. It was not exercised in a paid native model session. Improved generated status
wording or planning quality is **not demonstrated**. B-181/B-182 remain Open, and
B-169/C is not delivered by this change.

The demonstrated improvement is the actual read-only CLI answer on existing files:
closed live statuses are visible and three real epic mismatches are explicit, without
a refresh or the invalid mixed-entry predicate. This satisfies B-043's live-versus-
projected reporting contract; it does not certify arbitrary narrative freshness.
